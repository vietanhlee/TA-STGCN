Abstract

While municipal Closed-Circuit Television (CCTV) surveillance camera networks offer high-coverage visual feeds across smart cities, few benchmarks jointly evaluate camera-level vehicle count perception and network-wide spatio-temporal graph forecasting under motorcycle-dominant mixed traffic. We introduce a unified two-stage framework evaluated on a real-world city-scale benchmark of 608 active nodes in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam over an 86-day observation window (24,768 timesteps).

Stage 1 employs direct category-specific vehicle count regression (EfficientNet-B5) over 5,012 manually annotated traffic frames (MAE = 3.6363), eliminating expensive point-level annotations and per-camera calibration. Stage 2 evaluates TA-STGCN, a parameter-economical adaptation of STGCN that inserts Model-Level Temporal Self-Attention between spatial Chebyshev convolution blocks (K = 3). On clean inputs, graph forecasters achieve practically equivalent accuracy (MAE = 3.2401). However, under simulated camera perception noise, TA-STGCN degrades substantially more gracefully than non-attention graph/RNN baselines (Cohen's d up to +5.84 vs. GraphWaveNet at Level 4 noise), providing high noise robustness with a 70.4% parameter reduction over ASTGCN (~453K parameters).

Key Contributions

608-Node Benchmark

First city-scale, camera-only benchmark capturing chaotic mixed traffic from 657 CCTV cameras over 86 days (24,768 timesteps).

Direct Regression Counting

Stage 1 utilizes EfficientNet-B5 for direct category-specific count regression (5,012 images), eliminating per-vehicle point annotation costs.

TA-STGCN Model

Decouples Chebyshev spatial graph convolutions (K = 3) from temporal self-attention (h = 4), operating with only ~453K parameters.

Perception Noise Robustness

Demonstrates graceful error degradation under simulated camera noise (Cohen's d = +5.84 vs. GraphWaveNet at Level 4 noise).

Two-Stage System Architecture

Stage 1: Visual Perception

  • Input: 657 CCTV Feeds
  • Backbone: EfficientNet-B5
  • Task: Category Count Regression
  • Accuracy: MAE = 3.6363

5-Min Count Matrix

  • Resolution: 5-min Timesteps
  • Nodes: 608 Active Cameras
  • Matrix Shape: 24,768 × 608 × 2
  • Normalization: Z-Score Scaling

Stage 2: TA-STGCN

  • Spatial Conv: Chebyshev (K = 3)
  • Temporal Conv: GLU 1D Causal
  • Attention: Temporal Self-Attn (h = 4)
  • Parameters: ~453K (Lightweight)
